Proposed live connection and encryption

After signing, the contractor opens Payment setup from their agreement and verifies their identity through their verified contact. The server checks the signer and agreement on every request. Bank changes require fresh verification and a notification to the existing verified contact.

A payment provider would host the bank fields, encrypt submissions in transit and at rest, and return an opaque account reference and masked account details. The backend would link that reference to the signer and agreement. Full bank numbers must stay out of agreement PDFs, emails, logs and Command Center exports.

Authorized finance staff would see setup status, the account ending and submission history. Store the authorization version and timestamp with each submission. Verify provider confirmations on the server before changing status. Submission and bank verification are separate from issuing a payment.

Tax documentation

Kingdom Vibe, Inc. requests Form W-9 from U.S. contractors so we can identify the payee and prepare Form 1099-NEC when required. Reportable service payments are totaled across your agreements for the calendar year. An incorrect or missing taxpayer identification number may require backup withholding.

Form W-9 sample

Where applicable, the W-9 also records the LLC’s tax classification, other classification details, line 3b ownership information, and exempt payee and FATCA codes. Follow the form instructions for a disregarded entity’s owner name, classification and TIN.

The live workflow requires the applicable certification on the official form, including backup withholding status and a dated signature where required. The bank authorization is separate.
