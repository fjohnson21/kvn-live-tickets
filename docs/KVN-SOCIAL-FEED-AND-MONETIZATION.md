# KV Social — Fair Feed, Advertising, Boosts & Three Revenue Streams

**Status:** Proposed operating/product policy for review; does not enable ads, charge advertisers, alter production ranking, or authorize sharing sensitive member data.  
**Date:** 2026-10-14  
**Companion document:** [KVN Unified Platform Blueprint](KVN-UNIFIED-PLATFORM-BLUEPRINT.md)

## Product promise

**Organic opportunity is not pay-to-play.** Members should be able to grow through meaningful ministry, worship, testimony, encouragement, creative work and genuine relationships without paying for reach. KV Social must distinguish *content moderation*, *organic recommendation*, and *paid placements*, rather than silently reducing organic distribution to create demand for boosts.

Algorithm priorities:
1. Following relationships and member-selected interests.
2. Genuine viewer satisfaction and useful conversation — not outrage, controversy, vanity followers or raw comment volume.
3. Freshness, diversity, community fit, and a fair test for new/small accounts.
4. Safety, rights and privacy; transparent explanations and creator controls.
5. Revenue only in **separately labeled paid inventory**, with frequency and spending caps.

Our three commercial revenue categories (defined in prior KVN planning):

| Stream | Customer and source | Recognition rule |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Professional/profile subscriptions** | Ministry, business, artist and other professional workspaces; enhanced directories, sites, links, CRM and business tools. | SaaS subscription revenue net of concessions/refunds; keep payment processing, included service allowance and tax separate. |
| **2. Advertising** | **Profile boosts**, **direct corporate sponsorship/ads**, and **programmatic ads** when demand, provider approval and trust controls justify them. | Charge for eligible delivery/campaign products and record actual impressions, unique reach, clicks, conversion and refunds separately. One feed impression can satisfy only one advertiser's billable unit. |
| **3. Affiliate and venture commissions** | External affiliate referral offers plus participating KAP ventures such as **LuxRe Living**, **Set Apart Resources Co.**, and other agreed ventures. | **10% of the contractually eligible attributed revenue** for each participating KAP venture, NOT 10% of all venture sales, rent, deposits, pass-through charges or charitable giving. Confirm attribution window, exclusions, collection and refund rules. |

Ticketing and app apparel margins, Live event sponsors, eligible commercial platform transaction fees, and optional new premium services may generate **additional ecosystem revenue**. Do not automatically blend these into the three KV Social revenue lines, double count transaction fees, or classify charitable donations as KVN income.

### Reconcile prior financial model

The October 11 KVN business packet assumed:
- 20 *eligible ad opportunities* per monthly active user (MAU) per month — capacity illustration, not guaranteed or mandatory ad exposure.
- Professional profile ad buyers and corporate direct ads consume the same available ad inventory before programmatic buyers.
- An illustrative profile-ad budget of $100/month for some paid accounts; direct corporate contract examples at $5,000/month; and programmatic fill/yield assumptions. All are **forecast hypotheses**, not rate card commitments or contracted pipeline.
- Profile revenues were modeled at an older blended base price; the later proposed **KV Community / Creator / Launch / Growth / Pro / Network** packages require an explicit updated revenue forecast. Do **not** stack the old profile model on the new plans.

Actual eligible inventory is: **minimum of member-generated ad opportunities and governed placement/frequency caps**, reduced by sold direct inventory, exclusions, invalid traffic and member ad preferences. If there is no advertiser, show organic content — never show filler advertisements or suppress organic posts.

## 1. Member feed choices

**Following:** Chronological posts from accounts and approved organizations the member deliberately follows; no paid boosts inserted between followed posts. A followed account isn't artificially demoted for not purchasing promotion. Retain the ability to mute/unfollow/block and optionally filter by posts/media.

**For You:** Personalized, but explainable organic recommendations including followed sources, topically relevant community activity, new creators, and approved businesses/ministries. Introduce limited, clearly marked **Sponsored** ads inside reserved paid inventory (never hidden as regular organic posts).

**Discover:** Member-controlled exploration by content types and non-sensitive discovery filters, including artist, ministry and business directories, official events, recency and community topics. May have separate labeled discovery sponsorship placements with their own caps.

Member controls: Following/For You/Discover switcher, chronological preference, "Why am I seeing this?", Interested/Not Interested, Hide ad, Report ad, mute, block, topic tuning, and Data Saver/autoplay settings. Do not quietly reset their choices.

## 2. Organic scoring (initial rule-based version)

Start with a **simple, deterministic, auditable rule system**, not an opaque recommendation ML model. The weights below are *prototype constants* for A/B testing, not final proven ranking.

- 30% relationship signal: followed author, direct positive conversation, recent authentic interaction.
- 25% user-chosen relevant topic and content match (excluding inferred sensitive attributes).
- 20% quality and member satisfaction: meaningful watch completion, saves, replies with varied participants, fewer quick hides; normalizing across post length.
- 15% freshness and timeliness: relevant recent posts, upcoming official events.
- 10% diversity and equitable exposure: avoid repeat dominating authors, reserve exploration trials for new or small genuine accounts.

Do not expose counts of raw likes as a simplistic ranking objective. Rate-limit engagement abuse and detect coordinated brigading rather than reward it. Organic rankings never include subscription tier, ad spend, boost status or payments as feature inputs. A boost receives **additional labeled paid delivery only** while the original post remains organically eligible.

**Fair test for new creators:** A small sample of suitable consenting adult viewers who requested relevant content can encounter a newly approved post; recommend it wider only if real satisfaction indicators are good. No automatic promise of 100% follower delivery and no guaranteed virality.

Safety restrictions are disclosed categories (spam, impersonation, hateful attacks, abuse, fraud, copyright violations, doxxing, restricted content), applied independently of payment status, with an appeal path. Respectful criticism, denominational differences or low popularity are not reasons to suppress content.

## 3. Paid inventory and boost rules

**Initial guardrail:** 90%+ organic feed items in For You and Discover, with **no more than one sponsored or boosted item per 10 displayed feed cards**, combined across all paid buyers. This cap is a proposed maximum, not an ad-delivery target. Aim lower (e.g. one in 12–15) at launch, and stop filling when ads are not useful. Following contains no inserted paid boosts.

Additional frequency limits: default <= 2 exposures per paid creative/person/day and <= 6/week (to be validated per ad unit). Prevent the same advertiser from dominating a session. Disallow placing paid ads adjacent to sensitive prayer requests or crisis support unless approved by content policy.

### Boost workflow
1. Verified organization or established approved creator creates a compliant organic post or video.
2. Self-service "Boost Post" opens the organization workspace and shows eligible placements, estimated non-guaranteed reach, duration, spending cap, creative preview and disclosure.
3. Review advertiser identity, payment method, content/claim rules, member consent and any restricted category.
4. Boosted impressions occupy **labeled Sponsored inventory**; original post retains its ordinary organic score, even after the campaign ends.
5. Meter eligible visible impressions, invalid/bot traffic exclusion, clicks, profile visits, event/product leads and attributable conversions; export a ledger and reconcile payment.
6. Provide pause, reporting, refund/credit policy, budget exhaustion handling and dispute review.

**Example offer hypotheses only:** $10 entry boost, $25 featured campaign, $100 organizational campaign; define budgets versus service fees explicitly. Do not sell promised views or clicks. For a small audience, cap sellable inventory before charging for a campaign.

**Direct corporate ads:** Ad accounts undergo identity/creative review and enter a campaign approval queue. Reserve inventory on real delivery forecasts, not hypothetical MAU. For nonexclusive sponsorships, label the advertiser. Brand sponsorship ≠ ownership of an organic feed category.

**Programmatic inventory:** Add only after audience demand, compliance, privacy/cookie choices, valid supply-chain requirements, placement suitability and observed fill/yield justify it. Programmatic uses eligible *remaining* slots, never adds ad density beyond the global cap.

**Transparency:** Label paid posts **Sponsored** or **Advertisement** prominently on card/thumbnail/video where necessary. Include "Why this ad" and a sponsor identity. "Featured" or "Promoted" alone may be unclear. FTC guidance: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/native-advertising-guide-businesses

## 4. Respect faith-community privacy

KVN must not infer a member's personal faith, denomination, prayer subject, health situation, family crisis, donation amount or counseling activity for individualized ad targeting or advertiser segmentation. Religious beliefs are sensitive data for personalized advertising. Contextual placement by the *content/category of a public directory, event, post or page* and coarse geography may be preferable, but context cannot be repurposed to make sensitive user profiles.

- No targeting built from private prayer requests, counseling, private messages or member giving histories.
- No advertiser access to identifiable membership lists absent independently valid, specific permission and safeguards.
- Organization marketing audiences must be based on their own explicit opt-in, not merely a social follow, ticket purchase or community membership.
- Exclude minors from personalized ads; consider age-appropriate ad protections from day one.
- Apply local rules and provider requirements to employment, housing and financial ads; this matters if LuxRe campaigns feature housing.
- Publish a plain-language privacy and ad-personalization notice.
- Do not retarget a member because they expressed religious beliefs; sensitive-data targeting restrictions vary by ad provider and law.

Reference: https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/16701958?hl=en

## 5. Architecture: separate ranker, ad decisioning and accounting

**Request → Authorize viewer → Retrieve organically eligible candidates → Rank organic posts → Reserve bounded paid slots → Select approved ads separately → Frequency/privacy/safety checks → Render clearly labeled feed.**

The organic recommender sees NO ad budgets or account plan tier; paid candidate selection sees only explicitly allowed campaign targeting and available slots. Moderation/eligibility applies to both.

Suggested data objects:
- feed_preferences, follows, topic_preferences, content_impressions, watch_events (aggregated and privacy-bounded), content_hide_events, recommendation_decisions.
- advertiser_accounts, ad_campaigns, ad_creatives, boost_orders, campaign_budget_ledger, ad_delivery_events, ad_frequency_caps, fraud_adjustments, ad_invoices.
- creator_subscriptions, organization_subscriptions, feature_entitlements, usage_meters.
- affiliate_programs, referral_links, referral_clicks, attributed_conversions, eligible_revenue_adjustments, commissions_pending, commissions_confirmed, payout_reconciliations.

Protect ad payments and KAP venture commissions from double counting. Every delivery/commission record needs a provider attribution id, legal recipient, event timestamp, transaction state, refund state, and audit actor where appropriate. If KV Social sends a visitor to KV Tix, keep order/ticket funds and Disciple payout logic authoritative in KV Tix.

## 6. Performance, trust and member transparency

**Member health:** 7/30-day returning users, satisfaction prompts, number of engaged distinct creators, meaningful reply and save rates, report/hide rate, time to first relevant post.

**Fairness:** paid vs free creator reach on organic surfaces for comparable quality/age/follower counts; share of organic impressions to small or recently established profiles; consecutive same-author cards; variance by topic and session.

**Advertising:** eligible ad opportunities, actually sold/served ads, unique viewers, valid viewable impressions, frequency distribution, boost spend, conversions and refunds; monitor spam, complaints and bounce rate from ad landing pages.

**Commissions:** attributed referrals, eligible collected revenue, pending/confirmed commissions, negative adjustments/refunds and cash receipts, separated by venture and third-party program.

**Critical abuse checks:** fake follower/view rings, fake click farms, self-referral fraud, artificial content inflation, screenshot/thumbnail misuse and false organizations. Allow appeals of algorithmic or moderation restrictions. Prefer human review for consequential decisions.

Creator dashboard should offer:
- organic reach, nonfollower discovery, repeat viewers, watch completion, shares/saves and visit/conversion funnel;
- paid reach and ad attribution **separately**;
- explanation for distribution limitations (policy review, age/geo restriction, exhausted campaign, technical processing), without exposing anti-abuse secrets;
- no coercive "pay to restore your reach" prompts.

## 7. Development/rollout order

1. **KV Social private beta first:** chronological Following, lightweight topic-based For You/Discover, measurable reach and member feedback; keep ads OFF initially to validate genuine participation, bot controls, media and moderation.
2. **Paid account entitlements & boost simulator:** test-mode advertising accounts, sample budgets and frequency caps with zero live charging.
3. **Direct approved profile boosts + founding sponsor campaigns:** only after valid impressions, report exports and support; cap inventory; label Sponsorship.
4. **Affiliate links/commissions:** start with named participating KAP ventures under written 10% eligible-revenue agreements and a reconciliation ledger, plus approved external programs.
5. **Professional subscriptions and real paid campaigns:** activate after subscription billing, cancellations, usage limits, legal notices and org permissions work.
6. **Programmatic ads last:** only after compliance, verified delivery metrics, and material demand; do not force ad impressions to meet old spreadsheet assumptions.

Video-first short posts and scheduled Go Live remain planned; neither should require uninterrupted autoplay of advertisements. Ad video autoplay follows the same member controls and privacy/safety restrictions.

**Acceptance requirements before paid launch:** reproducible ranking tests, paid-slot cap tests, organic neutrality with/without a boost, explicit Sponsored labeling, banned/suspended advertiser blocking, click/view fraud resistance, sensitive-targeting restrictions, correct invoice/commission ledger, boost pause/refund tests, member feedback and ad controls, privacy and legal approval.

## Decision needed

Adopt as default policy:
- **Organic first / maximum 10% paid inventory** (starting lower).
- **Chronological, no-boost Following** plus transparent For You and Discover.
- **Three revenue lines separately reported**: professional subscriptions; direct/profile/programmatic ads including boosts; affiliate commissions including 10% of eligible attributed KAP-venture revenue.
- **Do not manipulate organic reach to sell boosts.**

Pricing, final quotas, first ad customer, legal entity and live rollout date require future approval. This document is guidance, not a production ad switch.
